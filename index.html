GETTING HERE

Travel Notes

BY TRAIN

Nagercoil Junction
approx. 15 km from the church. The main hub for trains from Chennai, Bengaluru and Thiruvananthapuram.
Valliyur
approx. 7 km from the reception hall, on the Tirunelveli–Nagercoil line.

BY AIR

Thiruvananthapuram International
approx. 75 km from the church. The closest airport, with taxis available at the terminal.
Thoothukudi
approx. 80 km from the reception hall, for domestic arrivals.

BY ROAD

To the church
On State Highway 91 at Konnakuzhivilai, a short drive from Nagercoil.
To the reception
Kallikulam sits on the Valliyur–Thiruchendur road. The Kallikulam bus stop is minutes from the hall.

BETWEEN THE TWO

Church to reception hall
approx. 50 km · around 1 hour 15 minutes by road. There is a long gap between the morning ceremony and the evening reception, so there is no need to rush.
Parking
Available at both the church and the hall.

Travelling from far? Do let us know — we would love to help you find your way.

We look forward to celebrating with you.
